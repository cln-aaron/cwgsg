What we're building

A platform to bring your AI tools together

Alongside our client work, we are building a product of our own. It puts the AI a business uses in one place it can govern, deciding which model handles each request and giving you a clear view of what is happening and what it costs.

How it works

Three steps, no rebuild

01

Connect

Point CWG at the models and the data sources you want to use. We handle the accounts, keys and plumbing in one place.

02

Set the rules

Decide who can do what, which data each team can reach, and where requests are allowed to go. Defaults are sensible from day one.

03

Build and ship

Your teams work through one interface or one API. Add a model later and nothing downstream needs to change.
