Highlights from Data Centre Asia 2026

CWG Innovations joined Asia’s premier data-centre and AI-infrastructure event in Hong Kong, where our Regional Director for APAC, Aaron, made the case for unified, secure AI — on stage and on the show floor.

Event recap8–9 July 2026Hong Kong

Data Centre Asia 2026, Hong Kong

CWG Innovations exhibited at Hall 3G · Booth 3G-D07 at the Hong Kong Convention & Exhibition Centre, one of Asia’s largest gatherings for data-centre and AI infrastructure. Event website →

Across two days on the show floor, our team met operators, enterprises and public-sector leaders from across the region — showing how RedRelay.ai brings frontier models, autonomous agents and the security that governs them into a single, sovereign-ready platform.

Keynote

Unified AI: The Convergence of Frontier Capability, Security, and Efficiency

Aaron, Regional Director, APAC · 9 July 2026

In his session, Aaron argued that most organisations now run AI across a dozen different models, vendors and tools — and that the bills, the keys and the risk are multiplying just as fast. As AI shifts from answering questions to taking actions, he warned, every new agent, model and integration becomes a fresh attack surface to defend.

His central point: security can no longer be bolted on at the edge. When an agent can act on your systems, security has to live inside the platform itself — and that is best achieved through convergence, not sprawl.

Key points from the session:

  • AI sprawl is the hidden cost — a dozen models, vendors and tools multiply spend, keys and risk in parallel.
  • In the agentic era, every new agent, model and integration is a new attack surface.
  • Convergence over sprawl: one unified platform where hundreds of frontier models, autonomous agents and their security run as a single layer.
  • A built-in AI security layer enforces least privilege between agent, model and data.
  • Hundreds of models route without lock-in, and production-ready agents drop in within days.
  • Smart routing cuts cost rather than inflating it — the organisations that win are the ones securing AI by design.
Panel

On the panel

Beyond the keynote, Aaron also took part in a panel discussion with fellow industry leaders, continuing the conversation on how organisations can adopt AI at scale without losing control of cost, security or sovereignty.
