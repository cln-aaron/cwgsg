Speaking session

Unified AI: The Convergence of Frontier Capability, Security, and Efficiency

Aaron, Regional Director, APAC · 9 July, 11:00–11:25

Most organisations are now running AI across a dozen different models, vendors and tools, with the bills, the keys and the risk multiplying just as fast. And as AI shifts from answering questions to taking actions, every new agent, model and integration becomes a fresh attack surface to defend.

Securing AI is no longer a problem you can solve after the fact, or bolt on at the edge. When an agent can act on your systems, security has to live inside the platform itself. This session makes that case through convergence: one unified platform where hundreds of frontier models, autonomous agents and the security that governs them run as a single layer rather than a dozen competing ones.

Drawing on real deployment patterns, it shows how a built-in AI security layer enforces least privilege between agent, model and data, how hundreds of models route without lock-in, and how production-ready agents drop in within days — all while smart routing cuts cost instead of inflating it. The takeaway: in the agentic era, convergence beats sprawl, and the organisations that win are the ones securing AI by design.
