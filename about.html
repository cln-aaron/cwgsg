About us

A Singapore AI company, built for trust

CWG Innovations builds tailored AI solutions and AI-driven cybersecurity, and the unified platform behind them, RedRelay.ai. We help organisations across Asia adopt AI quickly, and run it safely.

Why we exist

The upside of AI, without the exposure

Every organisation wants what AI promises. Far fewer can adopt it without risking their data, their compliance or their security.

We were built to close that gap: intelligent solutions and the trust to run them — on the cloud, or fully air-gapped in your own country.
