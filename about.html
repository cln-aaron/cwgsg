About us

A focused team with a clear job

CWG Innovation builds AI solutions and AI-driven cybersecurity. Our job is simple: help businesses adopt AI quickly, and run it safely.

Our story

We got tired of the gap

We watched businesses rush to adopt AI, then scramble to secure it. To us, building intelligence and protecting it were never two separate jobs.

So we started CWG Innovation to do both at once: build intelligent solutions and the security around them, and stay close enough to see them through.
