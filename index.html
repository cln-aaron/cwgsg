Where we go deep

Built around industries, not features

We pick a field, learn how the work really happens, then build agents that carry real weight in it. Three of those focuses are front and centre today.

Education

A place for students and lecturers to build real AI together

We are building a complete learning ecosystem where students across disciplines build agentic AI solutions for their own field of study, whether that is fintech, manufacturing or marketing communications. Lecturers work in the same space, giving feedback and shaping projects as they take form, so the learning and the building happen side by side.

Alongside it we are building tools that read classroom video and academic performance signals and turn them into insight for teachers. Grounded in sound pedagogical principles, the system helps educators strengthen differentiated instruction, see how each class is really doing and make classroom time work harder for every learner.

Cybersecurity

Security that runs inside the platform

Every agent you build is tested as it is built. An autonomous validation agent probes your work continuously, reasons like an attacker within the scope you set and closes the loop from finding a risk to fixing it, whether you run on our cloud or fully inside your own environment.

Fintech

Decision intelligence for capital and compliance

We build agents that take apart investment cases and sales performance, find the signal in the numbers and give investors and operators a clearer read before they commit. The same foundation powers AI-based AML and KYC checks, and deep organisational research that maps who really sits behind an entity and the risk that travels with them.

These are three of the fields we are taking furthest today. Others are already taking shape, built the same way and with the people and institutions who know each field best.
